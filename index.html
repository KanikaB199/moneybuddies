How it works

How a month works

  1. 1Everyone pays inEach member pays the same amount into the pot by the due date. The organiser collects it.
  2. 2Track who paid on timeThe organiser taps "Mark as paid". Everyone can see who paid on time, who is late and who has not paid yet.
  3. 3One member gets the potAt the end of the month, the full pot goes to the member whose turn it is. Next month, it is someone else's turn.

The organiser collects the money, as now. MoneyBuddies keeps a clear record that the whole group can see.
